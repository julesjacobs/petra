## Transfer family

All 12 corrected v2 source attempts completed: nine raw-automaton exports and
three 120-second frontend timeouts. The unavailable cases are the six-slot
path/cycle/chorded strict variants. The earlier v1 generator's 12 syntax failures
remain preserved. Finite-model tests and source serializability arguments do not
establish source-to-query translation correctness or an accepted backend proof.

The full 24-row pilot checks all six early-release cases with both legacy and
balanced portfolios. Both hit the sampled 2 GiB limit on each four-slot strict
case: `transfer_n4_path_strict`, `transfer_n4_cycle_strict`, and
`transfer_n4_chorded_strict`. Each definitive result has an independent
original-query witness check. Export time is separate from the 60-second
input/check-inclusive solver deadline; per-stage negative/checker work is 10B.

Three separate diagnostics locate the memory failures in structural
component-game expansion. Last observed game sizes are 283,624 / 242,112 /
100,028 nodes for path/cycle/chorded, with most control markings distinct.
These are censored observations. Schema candidate queue exhaustion is not a
completeness result for the automaton Parikh image.

The subsequent full 24-row dense/compressed comparison has **no coverage gain**:
both check six positives, leave three strict queries Unknown, and retain three
unavailable exports. Dense memory-limit failures become compressed solver
phase timeouts. A proposed stronger negative-proof mechanism remains research
work; this catalog supplies no evidence that it solves these cases.

## Walk gap recovery

The direct pilot checks all nine selected TokenRing positive gaps with each of
three preregistered seeds: 27 checked positives across 36 rows, versus 0/9 for
matched batched search. The integrated warmup pilot checks 9/9 versus 0/9 across
18 rows. All definitive results have saved independent original-input and
original-net witness checks. Seeds are reported separately; none was selected
by its outcome.

These nine queries are disjoint from the four 300-second survivors. The walk
pilots neither resolve those survivors nor establish full-cohort regression
coverage. Their five-second original-input solver budget excludes the separate
60-second independent check, unlike the raw input/check-inclusive deadline.
Walk firing counts and baseline distinct-state counts are different work units.
Do not pool the timings or compare them directly with earlier Linux runs.

Evidence: `walk-gap-pilot-v1/verification.json` and
`walk-portfolio-gap-v1/verification.json`, with their registered plans. Both are
selected local development experiments; broader Linux evaluation and ablations
need their own audited results.

All 22 reserved families remain excluded; no reserved payloads or results were
read. There is no pooled score, held-out claim, general-superiority claim or
publication-readiness claim. Keep complete parents and operational failures in
all subsequent comparisons.
