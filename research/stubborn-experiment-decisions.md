# Stubborn-set experiment decisions

The conservative visibility/freshness variant is retired from active performance development. Its complete strict screen solved40/104 versus42/104 for focused search, and its16-query diagnostic solved4 versus6. The apparent FastForward gain did not repeat and its profile showed no pruning. These observations justify directing engineering effort elsewhere; they do not establish a stable performance regression across the full population.

The registered624-row conservative repetition experiment is explicitly deferred indefinitely, rather than silently omitted. Its plan, frozen binary, source, results and diagnostic profiles remain unchanged and reproducible. No stable gain or speedup claim will be made from that experiment. Reopen the full repetition comparison if this variant is reconsidered for promotion.

The target-directed variant remains experimental. Its strict screen tied47/104, with three gains and three losses. Subsequent profiles show large reductions and direct witnesses on two MCC gain cases, together with expensive low-pruning selections on FastForward. Next work should preserve its sound selection argument while reducing selection overhead. Its separate624-row repeat plan remains registered and unlaunched; stable performance claims still require repetitions.
