# Audit review before confirmation

The first full audit checks the exact schedule, binary/script/input hashes, every saved artifact hash, per-row command, strict solver timing, bounded native checking, saved answers and validator responses, and agreement across methods and canonical duplicates. Its successful 736-row result supports the descriptive coverage figures on that frozen development cohort.

The new confirmation auditor additionally checks stage identities, exact terminal artifact file membership, row repeat/family/property-kind/collection metadata, saved validator requests against the query and original input, the DAG-work limit, and finite nonnegative validator times. These additions strengthen provenance consistency; they do not change the frozen first screen or reinterpret any of its results.

Remaining limits are explicit. Process-tree RSS and CPU measurements are sampled lower bounds, and CPU isolation is not enforced on the Mac. The audit reads the saved successful certificate-check evidence; it does not rerun every certificate. Execution receipts and artifact digests document integrity, not an independently observed proof of historical isolation. The first and optimized candidates must remain distinct comparisons.

Confirmation reports coverage first, with every family visible. Mean solver PAR-2 uses the registered invocation budget and full denominator. Actual solver plus validation time measures the end-to-end work of the run; common-solved timing is explicitly selected and cannot replace coverage. A larger validator cost must be visible even when solver timing improves.
