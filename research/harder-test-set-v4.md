## Adaptive projection did not improve coverage

The completed 16-row pilot compares structural and adaptive projection on four
queries at two work allowances, with the same frozen binary and 30s/2GiB limits.
Both solve 2/4 at 20 million work and 3/4 at two billion. All ten definitive
answers have successful independent checks, with no disagreement. n5 remains
unresolved: structural projection hits memory, adaptive projection hits work.

Single-place refinement can introduce artificial growth by omitting other places
in a conserved control component. On n3, the single-place game reached 49,576
nodes before its slice deadline; the structural fallback needed 756 nodes.
The subsequently implemented bounded-group variant also has no coverage gain
in its24-row pilot. Exact control sharing then completes n5's game at10B work;
streaming output emits its complete Rust-checked proof. Sparse duplicate-node keys
then let independent checking finish: the end-to-end60s/2GiB diagnostic accepts
n5 in40.4s at1776MiB. The same streaming binary with the previous checker exceeds
memory at the same limits. Earlier30s failures remain preserved. See `raw-adaptive-groups-pilot-v1-report.md`,
`raw-control-sharing-pilot-v1-report.md`, `raw-proof-streaming-pilot-v1-report.md`
and `raw-checker-sparse-identity-v1-report.md`. The completed full-cohort and scheduling follow-ups below supersede the earlier
unresolved classification without changing its saved rows.
