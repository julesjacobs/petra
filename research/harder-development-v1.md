## Evaluation discipline

Keep all eight reserved MCC families outside development. A held-out test must be selected independently of solver outcomes and frozen before final tuning. Hard outcome-selected development views diagnose weaknesses; the complete application suites and later reserved evaluation establish performance. Frontend/export, parsing/preprocessing, solving and checking costs retain their documented boundaries.
