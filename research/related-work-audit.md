# Related-work audit: current reachability portfolio

2026-09-27. Read-only audit during benchmark runs; no solver, tests, builds, or incomplete-run analysis. Findings concern the current causal-count refinement, delete-relaxed witness search, sparse exact states, and necessary-local-target threshold closure. They do not establish novelty priority.

**Assessment:** the individual mechanisms have close precedents. The current evidence supports a promising implementation, not yet a new general reachability algorithm or a publication-ready superiority claim. The most plausible narrow contribution is an efficient, independently checked combination of query-dependent local negative proofs and enabling-aware witness search, if ablations show that the interaction or certificate architecture adds substantial value. At present those engines largely operate as a portfolio; a common refinement mechanism has not been demonstrated.

## What the current local proof adds—and does not

For `sum a_i m_i >= b`, if every omitted `a_i <= 0`, nonnegative markings satisfy `sum_selected a_i m_i >= sum_all a_i m_i`. Consequently, projecting away those terms gives a necessary target. Projection removes enabling obligations and thus overapproximates executions. A checked threshold closure avoiding the necessary target proves the original target unreachable. Equality is handled as two inequalities, with the implementation’s checked-negation restriction.

This short soundness argument is elementary abstract interpretation; the sign test itself is not a credible main novelty claim. The potentially useful artifact is a compact original-net-checkable certificate that requires neither trusting component discovery nor shipping the explored global state space. The current development result is a reported 103-state certificate for the remaining JoinFreeModules diagnostic; it was not rechecked during this audit. That one case demonstrates utility, not a general advantage. Sparse exact state storage is likewise an implementation technique whose contribution should be measured in bytes, allocations, and instructions.

## Concrete publication gates

1. Freeze the candidate before evaluating held-out families. Current development success and the final local diagnostic were involved in design; neither establishes generalization. Repeated full runs, no disagreements, and compatible original-input budgets remain necessary.
2. Add FastForward and LoLA where input/target semantics can be matched. Keep unrestricted VerifyPN, its trace configuration, and SMPT’s strengthened/full configurations distinct. Separate short startup-dominated budgets from longer budgets; pairwise common-solved instruction ratios do not erase timeout bias.
3. Ablate relaxed guidance, helpful-action priority, lazy evaluation, sparse storage, causal refinements, and local closure. Measure solved cases, search expansions, certificate/checking cost, and memory/instructions. A portfolio union of separately successful answers is not a measured integrated solver.
4. Establish a coherent contribution: for example, use failed local closures or failed relaxed plans to select checked refinements in one shared abstraction, then show why this beats the independent stages. This is a proposal, not an implemented or novel result.
5. Before a theory claim, compare exact formulas against Metric-FF, Wimmel–Wolf, and polyhedral/slicing methods. Before a tool-paper claim, demonstrate reproducible held-out advantages and a small explicit trusted checker boundary. No present evidence warrants “record-breaking” or “publication ready.”
