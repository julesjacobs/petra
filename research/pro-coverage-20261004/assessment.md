## Independently assessed mathematics

For a weighted P/T net, a word executable from u remains executable from u+z
for nonnegative z, ending at v+z when it originally ended at v. Every preset
inequality remains true and the same transition effect preserves z. Repeated
application permits concatenating x_i copies of words u -> v_i from g*u when
sum x_i = g, ending at sum x_i*v_i. Overlapping pre/post arcs retain read guards.
This validates the composition principle without an independence assumption on
places or synchronized components. It does not apply unchanged to inhibitor or
reset extensions.

Signed target rows and equalities must be evaluated exactly on the final sum;
monotonicity of executions does not imply monotonicity of target satisfaction.
An unsuccessful divided search or infeasible sampled-endpoint master proves
nothing about original unreachability. Equality bounds need not divide g for
heterogeneous composition. Empty words represent unused copies.

The RC12 arithmetic is internally consistent: two signatures (1,0,0,0,1) and
three (0,0,1,0,0) sum to (2,0,3,0,2), and 2*181+3*1610=5192. Given the claimed
conserved unit block containing p1166 and p1211, uniform fivefold repetition
cannot meet the two positive requirements. The conserved block and actual
component execution claims have not been independently recomputed here.
